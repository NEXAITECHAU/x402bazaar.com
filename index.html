How x402 works

Step 1

Discover

Browse 27,000+ x402 services by category, price, or network. No sign-up needed.

Step 2

Call (free or paid)

GET a free endpoint with no auth, or POST a paid endpoint and receive HTTP 402 Payment Required.

Step 3

Sign payment

Your wallet (MetaMask, Phantom, etc.) signs an EIP-3009 transferWithAuthorization for the price. No approvals, no allowances.

Step 4

Retry with X-PAYMENT

POST again with the signed payment in the X-PAYMENT header. Service verifies, runs your request, and settles USDC to the seller in ~200ms.

Step 5

Done

You get the result. Seller gets paid. No invoices, no subscriptions, no credit cards.
